Your trust matters.

Protecting your private information is our priority. This policy applies to dareministries.org and to Dare Ministries.

What we collect

This website does not use accounts or advertising trackers. We use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how visitors find and use the site (for example which pages are viewed and roughly what region visitors come from). Analytics cookies are off until you click Accept on the cookie banner; you can change your choice any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer. If you accept, Google Analytics receives your IP address and basic device and browser information; we have turned off Google's advertising features and data sharing. You can also opt out with Google's browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Beyond that, we only receive personal information you choose to send us, such as your name, email address, phone number or mailing address when you email us, fill out a form, register for an event or give.

If you join an email list, such as updates for the Ireland internship or the Yet I Will Rejoice podcast, we store your email address with Mailchimp and use it only to send the updates you chose. Each list is separate, so you receive only what you signed up for, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

Forms and giving

Registration forms, newsletter sign-ups and online giving are handled by our church management provider, Breeze ChMS (dareministries.breezechms.com). Information you submit there is governed by their privacy practices as well as ours. Embedded videos are served by YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you press play.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, to provide the ministry services you ask for, to send updates you've signed up for and to issue giving receipts. We do not sell or rent your information.
