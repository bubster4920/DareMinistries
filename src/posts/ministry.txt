The vision of Dare Ministries is to prepare young Saints for a lifetime of ministry. So, what is ministry? What is the ultimate goal that we are striving for? What exactly are we preparing people for? Ministry is anything that is for the promotion of God’s coming kingdom. One day Jesus is going to return, and everything in this world is going to end. What does that mean for the time we have left? How are we able to impact the world for the glory of God through ministry?

Part of ministry is actively telling others about the Lord Jesus Christ. The good news of the Gospel is the truth that man has sinned and needs a Savior. Jesus saved all who believe at the cross, so ministry is for those who believe to tell others about what God has done for them through salvation. This is our ultimate calling as believers (Matthew 28:19-20). Part of the goal that we strive for is to train believers to be bold for what they believe and to tell this dying generation the good news of Jesus Christ. We hope for not only the boldness to share, but also the knowledge to share. Faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God, but if we don’t know the word of God, how are we supposed to share that with others?

Spiritual ministry goes so much further than just telling others about Jesus Christ; it involves discipleship. It involves edifying other believers and using our spiritual gifts from within the body of Christ. Upon believing in Jesus Christ, we are indwelled by the Holy Spirit and we receive our gift: the gift that we have is not something that is self-promoting or arrogant, but something that builds the body of Christ. Once saved, we become not only part of the body, but necessary for the body. Ministry is using what God has given us to edify others who believe. So, whether that be serving in our local churches, or praying for your brothers and sisters in Christ, whether you are preaching to large crowds, or your service is never seen by the eyes of men; regardless of what that gift may be, you are essential to the body and crucial for ministry.

I can’t tell you how many people believe that they have to get paid for their ministry in order for it to be ministry. There is vocational ministry: pastors, missionaries, and evangelists; but most ministry is not something that you do as your main means of support. In reality, the way that you live your life becomes ministry. The way that we live our lives reflects who Christ is (Matthew 5:16). That can be for the good or the bad. Working hard in whatever stage in life you find yourself becomes ministry. Whether that is as a student or working as a CEO for a Fortune 500 company, you are engaged in ministry by your example. People are watching: they see what you do, what you say, and how you work and all of that reflects Christ. No matter where God has you, He has called you to ministry by doing your absolute best. Ministry is not searching for recognition or eye service, but having the integrity to give 100% in the life and situation that you are in.

So, yes ministry is telling others about Jesus Christ, edifying and discipling other believers to be more like Christ, and working hard in whatever God has called you to reflect Christ. We are called to be like Christ in all that we do and in who we are. That therefore becomes ministry. Dare Ministries therefore exists to help believers develop their skills and gifts for the promotion of the Kingdom of God for the glorification of Christ. So part of our ministry is continuing to grow to develop ways for believers to reach others and serve others.
