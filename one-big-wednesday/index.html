One night. Every church.

One Big Wednesday is a free night of worship and the gospel for middle and high school students from churches all across the Front Range.

Visit onebigwednesday.org →

Come as you are. Bring a friend.

Students in grades 6–12 hang out, worship together and hear the gospel. It costs nothing to attend.

When & where

Wednesday, April 21, 2027, at the Norris Penrose Event Center in Colorado Springs. Doors open at 5 PM and the night wraps up at 9 PM.

What happens

Games, merch and concessions as doors open, then worship led by leaders from local churches, a gospel message, audience games and giveaways.

Who it's for

Students, parents and youth pastors. Churches are encouraged to register a group and bring their students together.

Dates, registration and details are managed by the One Big Wednesday team, so please check their site for the latest.

Life Essentials.

A free six-meeting guide for students who have just come to faith, covering Scripture, prayer, overcoming sin, the church, the Holy Spirit and sharing the gospel.

Download the booklet (PDF) ↓

What's inside

  • Six short meetings, each with a video, verses and questions
  • Next steps after every meeting
  • Ends with how to share the good news

Get involved.

Register, sign up to serve, or learn how your church or business can take part, all at One Big Wednesday's own site.
